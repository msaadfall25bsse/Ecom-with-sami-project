### 🎬 Tab 1: Hero & Video Banner (`hero`)
*Controls the top hero area on the homepage (`/`).*

* **What you can change**:
  1. **Badge Tag**: e.g., `PAKISTAN’S #1 UAE & KSA DROPSHIPPING TRAINING`
  2. **Main Headline**: White text shown prominently at the top.
  3. **Highlighted Ending Text**: Text highlighted in Cyan Blue (e.g. `step-by-step training`).
  4. **Subtitle Description**: 2-line explanation beneath the headline.
  5. **Video Bar Title**: e.g. `Watch this 128 seconds video to learn how easy it is to start`.
  6. **Video Embed URL**: Paste any YouTube embed link (e.g. `https://www.youtube.com/embed/...`) or direct MP4 link.
  7. **Video Poster Thumbnail**: Image shown before clicking play. You can paste an image URL or click **"Upload"** to pick an image from your computer.
  8. **CTA Button Text**: e.g. `YES! I WANT TO LEARN THIS`.
  9. **Pricing Boxes**:
     - **Discounted Price**: `3,900 PKR`
     - **Original Price**: `32,500 PKR`
     - **Discount Percentage**: `88% OFF`
* **How to save**: Click **"Save Hero Changes"** at the top-right of the tab. Updates appear instantly on the live site!

### 🎥 Tab 6: Student Video Reviews & Proofs (`site_reviews`)
*Controls video testimonials, earnings screenshots, and student success stories.*

* **What you can do**:
  - Click **"+ Add New Student Review"**.
  - **Upload Video File Directly**: Click the cyan **"Upload Video"** button to upload video files (`.mp4`, `.webm`, `.mov`) directly from your computer/media without needing YouTube! You can also paste an external video link if preferred.
  - **Upload Thumbnail Screenshot**: Click the green **"Upload Image"** button to upload the student's earnings screenshot or video poster image.
  - Enter: Student Name, City (e.g. `Lahore`, `Karachi`), Market (`UAE Market`, `KSA`), Sales text (e.g. `AED 5,000 / Week`), Orders count (`56 Orders`), Student Quote, and Star Rating (1 to 5).
  - Edit or delete existing reviews at any time.
* **Playback Behavior on Live Website**:
  - Videos **never auto-play in the background**.
  - When a visitor clicks on any review or play button, the video plays smoothly with full player controls on demand!

### ✍️ Tab 9: Blog Articles & News Announcements (`site_blogs`)
*Allows publishing articles, educational guides, and marketing news directly to the public website.*

* **What you can do**:
  - Click **"+ Create New Article"**.
  - Enter: Article Title, Excerpt, Full Content, Featured Image URL (or Upload Image), Tags, and Publish Status (`PUBLISHED` vs `DRAFT`).
  - Edit or delete articles at any time.
* **Where it appears on the live website**:
  1. **Homepage Blog Feed**: Displayed in the dedicated "Latest Dropshipping Guides & Strategies" section at `http://localhost:3001/`
  2. **Public Blog Directory**: Complete article listing with search & category filters at `http://localhost:3001/blogs`
  3. **Full Reader Page**: Individual article with author bio and enrollment CTA at `http://localhost:3001/blogs/<article-slug>`

## 🛡️ 5. Tips & Best Practices
1. **Always Click Save**: After making edits in any tab, click the corresponding **"Save Changes"** button at the top-right of that tab.
2. **Instant Live Preview**: Open `http://localhost:3001/` in another browser tab to view your changes immediately without restarting the server.
3. **No Coding Required**: Store managers and non-technical staff can update prices, seasonal discounts, and announcements anytime with zero risk of breaking the code!
